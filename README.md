A single line in the Moon's voice, 148 characters. `main.py` writes it to
`/output/entry.txt`. The line was chosen from 16 candidates in a
criteria-ordered tournament (Moon answering wishes, howls and late-night
promises first; then funniest on one read; wry, old, tired-of-being-wished-upon
voice; a comeback turn before the line ends; no wasted characters) and refined
over four iterations: the Moon has kept receipts on four billion years of
wishes, howls and 2 a.m. promises — and the tide is still the only one who
shows up when it calls.
